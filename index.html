Triangle button — Tap the ⚠ on the right side of a task to report an issue or damage. A note and photo field will appear.

Amber-highlighted card — Especially important task, pay extra attention.

Detail
Quick / Detail toggle — Top-right corner. Quick hides step instructions. Detail shows them. Your choice is saved.
⏱
Dwell timer — In Phase 3 (Arrive & Prep), start it after spraying chemicals. Set the duration (5–20 min), then work on other tasks while chemicals soak. An alarm plays when time is up. You can mute the alarm using the toggle on the timer.
📸
Photo reminders — Take at least 5 pre-clean photos (bed, kitchen, CR, equipment close-up, utensils) and 5 after-clean photos when done. Use 📷 Capture or 🖼 Upload. Photos go directly to Telegram when you submit — no Messenger needed.
📦
Supply log — After cleaning, tap Log Supply Usage in the Clean phase. Log everyday items with +/− buttons; liquids in ml (auto-converted to units). Required before you can go to Finish.
🔒
Confirm & Leave — The last 4 checks before leaving. All must be confirmed or flagged with an issue. The checklist cannot be submitted until all 4 are done.
