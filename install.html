Install available Chrome detected the app — add it to your home screen in one tap.
1
Open the checklist in Safari
Tap the gold button below. The app must open in Safari — Chrome on iPhone cannot install apps to the home screen.
2
Tap the Share button
At the bottom of Safari, tap the Share icon — a square with an arrow pointing up.
📤 Share — bottom center of Safari, on the toolbar
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the share sheet. Tap "Add to Home Screen" — the option with a plus sign.
➕ Add to Home Screen — scroll down in the share sheet to find it
4
Tap "Add" to confirm
A preview shows the app name and icon. Tap Add in the top-right corner. The checklist icon will appear on your home screen.

Note: On older iPhones the Share button may appear at the top of the screen. Look for the same square-and-arrow icon.

1
Open the checklist in Chrome
Tap the gold button below. Use Google Chrome for the smoothest install experience.
2
Watch for the install banner
Chrome may show an "Add to Home Screen" or "Install app" banner automatically. Tap it to install immediately.
🔔 Install banner — appears near the bottom or top of Chrome automatically
3
Or use the Chrome menu
No banner? Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner of Chrome.
⋮ Menu — three vertical dots, top-right corner of Chrome
4
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install app" from the menu, then tap Install to confirm.

Samsung phone? Open in Samsung Internet → tap the bookmark icon → "Add page to" → "Home screen".

1
Open the checklist in Chrome or Edge
Tap the gold button below. Chrome and Edge both support PWA installation.
2
Click the install icon in the address bar
Look for a small monitor icon or download icon at the right side of the address bar. Click it to install.
🖥 Install icon — right side of the address/URL bar
3
Or use the browser menu
Click ⋮ menu → "Save and share" → "Install Cascade Hideaway Cleaning Checklist…"
4
Click "Install" to confirm
The app opens in its own window and a shortcut is added to your taskbar and desktop.
